Three taps from blink to keeper

You don't need to retake anything. Open Eyes works with the shots already in your gallery.

  1. Choose your photos

    The photo you want to fix and the shots to bring eyes and smiles from, ideally from the same photo shoot.

  2. Tap the photo to fix

    The other shots are where eyes and smiles come from.

  3. Tap a face to fix it

    Bring in better eyes or a better smile from the other shots. Faces that can look better are highlighted for you.

Your photos never leave your phone

Finding faces, lining up your shots and blending a fix all happen on the phone in your hand. There is no account and no upload, and this website doesn't track you either.

Fixed photos say they're fixed

A fixed photo is a composite of your own shots. Nothing is generated by AI: every eye and smile is a real moment from another frame. We still don't pretend it's the untouched original.

Each saved fix carries a standard "composite" label in its metadata, Content Credentials where your phone supports them, and an invisible watermark that survives most re-saving.
